+1 Range Shorts - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. +1 Range Shorts is a personal desktop tool operated by Daegal Magic (the developer) to upload short gameplay videos of the Roblox game "+1 Range, Slash Everything" to the developer's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

What we access: when you sign in with TikTok and approve access, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (display name) for the account that signs in. It also reads the video file you choose to upload and the caption and privacy settings you enter.

How we use it: only to upload the video you choose to the TikTok account you authorized. Nothing else.

Storage and sharing: the access token is stored only on the developer's own computer. We do not run a server, we do not collect or store data about other people, and we never sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, other than sending the video you chose to TikTok.

Revoking access and deleting data: you can revoke the tool's access in TikTok at any time (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). Deleting the token file from the computer removes all stored data.

Children: the tool is not directed to children and is not offered to the public.
